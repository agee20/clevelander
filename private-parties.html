A Cozy, Stylish Atmosphere with Downtown Energy

Are you planning a special corporate event, sports viewing party, birthday, alumni mixer, or rehearsal dinner? The Clevelander Loft has everything you need to make hosting seamless, memorable, and cost-effective.

With two large panoramic picture windows overlooking the vibrant Gateway District, the Loft combines historic architectural charm, exposed brick, and warm wood accents with modern sports viewing amenities. Our friendly, seasoned staff will handle every detail of food and bar service so you can relax and enjoy your event.
